Who we are

A single desk for the whole journey.

Clay Next T LLC provides travel concierge and lifestyle services. We assist clients with travel arrangements, accommodation, transportation, event ticket sourcing, and other personalised booking and coordination services.

In practice that means you send one message. We compare, negotiate, book, confirm and follow the details through to the moment you arrive home — including the ones that change at the last minute.
